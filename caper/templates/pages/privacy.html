AmpliconRepository is an academic research resource developed and maintained by the Bafna Lab at the University of California San Diego. This page describes what information the site handles and why. It is short because the site collects little.

Account information

If you create or use an account, you may provide or associate information such as a username, an email address, other account or profile information, and the authentication information needed to sign you in.

Signing in through Google or Globus

You may sign in through a third-party authentication provider such as Google or Globus. When you do, AmpliconRepository receives the limited account or profile information that the provider makes available for sign-in — typically name or profile information and an email address, as applicable. AmpliconRepository does not receive or store your password for those providers.

How account information is used

Account information is used to operate the repository — to maintain your account and to support project ownership and membership, project access, notifications, uploads and submissions, API access, and other functionality you ask the site to perform.

Cookies and sessions

AmpliconRepository uses cookies and similar browser or session mechanisms that are necessary for the site to work: signing you in and keeping you signed in, security, cross-site request forgery (CSRF) protection, maintaining your session, and preventing abusive automated downloads or requests. The site does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

Anti-abuse and CAPTCHA services

Some actions, such as creating an account or requesting certain downloads, may use a third-party CAPTCHA or anti-abuse service to distinguish people from automated traffic. Those providers receive the technical information they need in order to provide that service.

Server and application logs

Operating the site produces logs that may record information such as your IP address, browser and user-agent information, the URLs requested, timestamps, referrer information, and technical or error information. These logs are used for security, troubleshooting, and operating the repository, and are retained for a limited period.

Project history and provenance

The repository keeps a limited history of submissions and projects — including records of who submitted or changed a dataset and when. This information is retained where it is needed for repository integrity, provenance, audit and history, security, and reproducibility of the scientific record. Because these records exist to describe what happened, they are generally preserved even after an associated account is closed.

Email

AmpliconRepository may send you functional messages related to your use of the site: account activity, project membership, repository updates for projects you follow, and downloads or processing you have requested. These are not marketing emails. You can adjust which notifications you receive on your {% if user.is_authenticated %}Settings page{% else %}Settings page once signed in{% endif %}.

API access

Signed-in users may be issued an API credential associated with their account so that scripts and pipelines can access the repository on their behalf. You can generate or revoke that credential yourself at any time.

No advertising, no sale of personal information

AmpliconRepository does not serve targeted advertising and does not sell users' personal information.

Questions, corrections, and account deletion

If you have a privacy question, need to correct your account information, or want your account deleted, email jluebeck@ucsd.edu. For anything that is not privacy- or account-specific — bugs, feature requests, and questions about the data — the GitHub issue tracker is the better place, and gets a faster answer.

See also the UC San Diego Website Privacy Statement for general institutional information, and this site's Terms of Use.
